Formats, memory & compatibility

WAV PCM 8/16/24/32-bit and float 32/64-bit, plus uncompressed AIFF / supported AIFF-C variants, are decoded directly. MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis/Opus, WebM and other audio types use your browser’s decoder. Support depends on both the codec and browser; not every file with these extensions will work. Copy-protected files are not supported. If decoding fails, try another browser or export WAV / AIFF.

For browser-decoded audio, the displayed rate is the decoded PCM rate (44.1 kHz), not necessarily the original file rate. Lossy formats may include encoder padding: check the preview if precise loop timing matters. Import does not restore details lost during MP3/AAC compression. Stereo averaging can cancel out-of-phase material; choose the left or right channel instead.

Output is mono 16-bit MIDI Sample Dump Standard, device ID 0, sample number 0, with looping disabled. The Machinedrum UW plays samples at 12-bit resolution. The preview here is the converted 16-bit audio, not an emulation of the UW.

This tool allows up to 1,000,000 output samples, 80 MiB input files and three minutes of source audio. The displayed PCM size is an estimate, not a measurement of free UW memory. Available space depends on your existing samples. Explicitly select the receive slot on your machine to avoid overwriting sounds.

No MIDI transmission, uploads, analytics or external services. Works offline with the downloaded files. Independent community tool, not affiliated with Elektron. Verify the final transfer on your hardware.
